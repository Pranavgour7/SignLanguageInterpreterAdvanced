How It Works

Three-stage ML pipeline running entirely in your browser

01
📷

Hand Detection

MediaPipe Hands detects and tracks 21 key landmarks on your hand in real-time from the webcam feed.

02
📐

Feature Extraction

The 42 landmark coordinates (x,y for each point) are normalized and reduced to 25 principal components via PCA.

03
🧠

Classification

A Random Forest ensemble of 100 decision trees votes on the sign, producing a real-time prediction with confidence scores.
